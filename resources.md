## How to Use This Guide

- Resources are organized by day to align with the learning plan
- Each resource is categorized by type: 📄 Paper | 📝 Blog | 🎥 Video
- Start with resources for your current day, but feel free to explore ahead
- Bookmark important resources for future reference
